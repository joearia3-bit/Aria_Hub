Aria Hub Help

1. Create a listing

  • Product or service name: a short, clear name, such as "Television".
  • Category: choose the closest match from the list.
  • Price (K): the amount in kina, numbers only, such as 300.
  • Location: the town or area where the item is available.
  • Description (optional): up to 300 characters. Include condition, brand, size, and how to arrange a viewing.

2. Post it

Tap Post Listing. Your listing appears under All listings.
